# Content policy

The framework publishes links, bibliographic facts, licensing notes, and original summaries

It does not redistribute a third-party course, book, slide deck, paper, dataset, or video unless the license explicitly permits redistribution

Resource selection must distinguish:

- free to access
- paid
- institutionally accessible
- redistributable

Free access is not the same as permission to republish

High-risk learning paths require an explicit safety boundary and human review; the framework does not replace professional, medical, legal, financial, licensing, regulatory, or operational authorization
